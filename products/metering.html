Capabilities

What's in it.

  • LUFS — momentary, short-term and integrated loudness, with a history graph.
  • True Peak — inter-sample peak detection, per channel.
  • Peak & RMS — fast and slow ballistics against a dBFS reference.
  • Goniometer — stereo correlation and mono compatibility at a glance.
  • Streaming targets — Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube and Tidal references built in.
  • Mac & Windows — AU, VST3 and AAX. Universal binary on Apple Silicon.
