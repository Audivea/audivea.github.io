The two-view split

One measurement, two ways to read it.

Arcoline reads everything once, then shows it at two densities — switch any time with Tab.

Dive

The full workbench.

Every reading on one screen — for when you're diagnosing a problem or running a mastering QC pass.

Ascend

The at-a-glance verdict.

One big loudness number and a ±LU delta against your target — the “am I safe?” answer, glanceable while you create.

What it measures

The whole delivery check, in one plugin.

Everything you'd otherwise open three meters to see — with built-in targets for Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, broadcast and film. The manual covers every reading in full.

Loudness

Momentary, Short-term and Integrated LUFS — the number platforms normalize to.

True peak

Inter-sample dBTP with a sticky over-flag, so a stray transient can't slip past.

Level

Per-channel peak & RMS with peak-hold and clip detection.

Dynamics

PSR, PLR and loudness range — how squashed or open your master is.

Stereo image

Goniometer, phase correlation and L/R balance — a fast phase-safety check.

Tonal balance

Low / mid / high energy split — catches a bass-heavy mix or a brittle top.

Made for your room

Three themes for any room.
